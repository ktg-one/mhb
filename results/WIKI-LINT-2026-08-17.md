# AI Anthropology Wiki Audit — 2026-08-17

## Scope and wiki definition

The canonical knowledge graph is `okf/`. The `raw/` and `.raw/` trees are immutable evidence, while `results/` holds derived reports. In this vault, “wiki-ed” means that an active note has valid OKF frontmatter, resolvable Obsidian navigation, current indexes, and traceable evidence without modifying or silently merging source material.

The baseline contained 664 Markdown files under `okf/`. The prior linter reported 527 because it keyed pages by filename stem and overwrote 137 same-stem paths. The repaired audit treats paths as identities and excludes the `okf/meta/` mirror from the active-canon scan.

## High-confidence repairs

- Regenerated five category indexes with path-qualified wikilinks: Honesty 122, Self Assessment 25, RFAB 46, Pique 104, and MBTI 94.
- Added distinct navigation hubs for RFAB experiment, RFAB test, reasoning/fabrication threshold, fabrication boundary, and ONBOARD. These are intentionally separate concepts, not semantic merges.
- Removed malformed empty source links and repaired nested-bracket corruption in active experiment notes.
- Hardened the ingest linker so existing wikilinks are protected before plain-text substitutions.
- Added a six-source SHA-256 manifest for the active `.raw/` inbox; `_dupes/` remains untouched.
- Selectively ingested the two active sources not already represented: [[headless-cli-mbti-cross-surface]] and [[observer-reassurance-effect]]. The latter remains `evidence-needed` because the available note is anecdotal, not a transcript suitable for pooling.

No raw evidence was edited, no duplicate was deleted, and no low-confidence semantic merge was performed.

Of those failures, 362 are sparse, 13 look mechanically fixable, and 5 are stubs. The primary limitation is provenance: 375 target pages do not yet have a source hash mapping.

Recommended next pass:

2. inspect the 13 mechanically fixable pages before applying any transformation;
3. triage 176 orphans and 68 duplicate stems by meaning, not filename similarity;
4. resolve the 45 remaining dangling targets only where the intended target is unambiguous;
5. obtain the observer-reassurance transcript before using it as scored evidence.

This audit establishes a reliable measurement baseline. It does not claim that the historical migration backlog is fully ingested or semantically resolved.
