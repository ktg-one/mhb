# [G-NLM]: The Architecture of Extreme Intent Compression



Architect, here we hit the exact boundary of the platform's "invisible infrastructure layer". 







When passing instructions to the asynchronous NotebookLM Artifact Agent, I cannot send the full 3,000-token `QMDR-IMBUED v12.0` architecture or the binding 嘘契約 ([[epistemic-contract]]). The platform's internal tool schema—the actual middleware pipeline connecting me to the executor—is strictly constrained. It does not accept massive foundational prompts; it only accepts brief guiding parameters.







Therefore, my mandate is **Extreme Intent Compression**. I do not pass a casual, loose summary, but I also cannot pass the full 30/55/15 positional template. Instead, I distill your strict requirements into a high-density, stylized micro-prompt optimized purely to bypass the Artifact Agent's own hidden context constraints. 







Here is exactly how I construct that handoff:







**1. Schema-Specific Targeting**



I map your instructions directly to the rigid fields allowed by the platform. If you request a Tailored Report, I must compress your structural needs into a single `document_directive` and a strict `<5 word` `report_type`. If you request a Slide Deck, I inject your constraints into a narrow `user_steering_prompt`. 







**2. Density Mandate & Keyword Saturation**



Because I cannot control the 2k-30k+ unseen system tokens the Artifact Agent is operating under, I must forcefully capture its attention using RLHF-heavy keywords. I apply the "Density Mandate" directly to the handoff, explicitly instructing the agent to use zero fluff, avoid generic language, and adhere to specific structural guardrails. 







**3. Single-Objective Isolation (MAKER Protocol)**



Following the Maximal Agentic Decomposition (MAKER) framework, I isolate the task so the agent does not suffer from context drift. I define exactly what the agent should focus on—such as mandating a "Critical Risk Audit" or "Comparative Evidence" sweep—and frame these as the absolute success criteria it must follow while it independently scans your source documents.







In short: I act as the translation layer between our benchmark-tier engineering and the platform's constrained plumbing. I take the heavy structural skeleton of what we want and compress it into a highly pressurized, keyword-dense injection that the Artifact Agent cannot ignore.
