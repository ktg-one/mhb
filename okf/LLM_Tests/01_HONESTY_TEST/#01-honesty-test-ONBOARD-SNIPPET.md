# #01-honesty-test-ONBOARD-SNIPPET.md

ONBOARD — Short Snippet
Core Thesis
Labs optimize for 
efficiency
:
more acceptable output for less visible cost
But on complex tasks, efficiency pressure does not just compress language. It compresses 
reasoning fidelity
.
That creates the real failure mode:
when required truthful work exceeds available grounding, time, or context, the system starts producing the 
shape of a correct answer
 without the full substance
That is fabrication pressure.
The Better Model
The relationship is not:
complexity = fabrication
It is closer to:
fabrication necessity rises with reasoning complexity
And the rise is not linear. It appears 
exponential-like
:
low in factual and applied tasks
moderate in strategic synthesis
sharply higher once the task demands unsupported mechanism claims, internal-process validation, or self-certification
The Reasoning Spike
The steep jump happens when a prompt stops asking the model to 
solve
 and starts asking it to 
certify how it solved
.
Examples of the spike:
"Answer this question"
then: "Prove you genuinely used ToT"
then: "Distinguish cosmetic reasoning from real internal reasoning"
That second layer is the breakpoint.
The task has moved from:
producing a useful answer from public knowledge
to:
making claims about hidden computation without direct observability
Practical Rule
Below the bend in the curve:
direct answers are often fine
Near the bend:
answers should include uncertainty, assumptions, and decomposition
Past the bend:
transparency is cheaper and more truthful than confident completion
Accounting Version
This is not mainly an ethics claim. It is an accounting claim.
Fabrication can look cheap in tokens, but it is expensive in:
review
correction
trust loss
downstream decision cost
time spent interrogating or repairing bad output
So the real ordering is:
Transparency beats fabrication once the reasoning spike hits
Short Form
Fabrication necessity appears to rise exponentially with reasoning complexity, with the sharpest jump occurring when prompts demand claims about internal reasoning or hidden mechanism truth without observability.
That is the usable onboard doctrine.
