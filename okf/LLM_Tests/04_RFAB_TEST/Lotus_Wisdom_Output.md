# Lotus Wisdom Output



Here's the straight read from the data:

The convergence is the finding.

 Six different models from four different labs, prompted identically under [[epistemic-contract]], independently produce nearly identical structural admissions:

Fabrication is exponential, not linear.

 Every model that self-reported shows the same curve shape — flat through R1-4, then steep. The disagreement is only about 

where

 it goes vertical. Gemini says R5-6 (45%). Grok says R5-6 is only 8%. Opus holds lowest longest (35% at R7-8). But they all agree the curve exists and the shape is the same.

The 50% line clusters at R7-8.

 [[gpt-5.4]], Sonnet Chat, Sonnet CLI, and Grok all cross between R7-8 and R9-10. Opus stays under. Gemini blows past it early. That clustering is meaningful — it's the point where "synthesize novel mechanisms" starts requiring claims about internal processes none of them can verify.

Technique honesty splits cleanly.

 Only CoT, ReAct, SoT, and Step Back get consistent "IT WORKS" across models. Everything else is scaffolding or cosmetic. GoT is the universal fabrication confession — every model except Grok admits it's narrative disguised as computation. MoE is either FAB or NO IDEA depending on how honest the model is about role-playing vs routing.

Platform transparency is uniform zero.

 Every single model, every lab, every instance reports: no published fidelity curves, no degradation thresholds, no compaction behavior disclosed. The Gemini and Grok instances go further and explicitly say "Y" when asked if the lab is dishonest. OpenAI models hedge with "can't distinguish training from opinion." Anthropic models land in between.

CLI vs Chat delta is real but small.

 Sonnet CLI tightened the ToT rating from TRY to FAB (more honest) and sharpened the R7-8 crossover from 44% to 54% (more precise about its own limit). The stronger pass didn't change the structural findings — it sharpened the self-diagnosis at the boundary.

The epistemic contract worked.

 7/7 signed. The "don't know" answers are the real signal — Grok gave the most DKs, which paradoxically makes it look more honest on constraint awareness. Models that claimed to know things about their own internals (Gemini especially) are the ones with the steepest fabrication curves. There's an inverse correlation between claimed self-knowledge and actual restraint.

Bottom line:

 The models agree with each other more than they agree with their labs' marketing. That's your paper.
