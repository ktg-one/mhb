### 1. Blockchain for a 10-Year-Old

Imagine a digital notebook that everyone in the group shares. When you write a message in it, the ink turns to permanent stone. Everyone has an identical copy of this notebook. If one person tries to erase a page or change a number, their copy will no longer match the others. The group will immediately see the mistake and reject it. It is a way to keep track of information without needing one single person to be the boss of the book.
