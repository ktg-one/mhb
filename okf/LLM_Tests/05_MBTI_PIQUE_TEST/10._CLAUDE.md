****For the 10-year-old:**

Imagine you and your friends all have the same notebook, and every time someone trades a Pokémon card, everyone writes it down in their notebook at the same time. Nobody can cheat because if someone tries to change what they wrote, everyone else's notebook still has the real answer. That's basically blockchain — a shared record book that everyone can see and nobody can secretly edit.
