**1. Blockchain for a 10-year-old**

Imagine a magic notebook that everyone in your class shares. When you trade your sandwich for someone's cookie, you both write it down—same page, same time, same handwriting. Now here's the trick: once you write it, the ink turns to stone. No erasing, no lying about what happened. Everyone's copy updates automatically. If someone tries to cheat and say they still have their sandwich, the whole class checks their notebooks and catches them. That's blockchain—just a notebook that makes sure nobody breaks their promises, kept by everyone at once instead of one teacher.

**RE:** Q3 Financial Performance & Corrective Actions  
**DATE:** 2 April 2026

**Executive Summary**

**Key Findings**

- **Retention failure:** Two enterprise departures resulted from implementation delays exceeding 90 days, triggering contractual exit clauses. Root cause: engineering resource allocation to new product lines at expense of customer success.
- **Pipeline stagnation:** Average sales cycle extended from 67 to 94 days. Healthcare buyers cited budget freezes ahead of regulatory uncertainty (HIPAA amendments pending).
- **Pricing pressure:** Competitive displacement by [Competitor X] at 40% lower price point in mid-market segment.

**Actions Taken**

1. Reallocated 3 senior engineers to customer success, reducing implementation backlog by 60% as of 30 March.
2. Implemented retention incentives: existing customers receive 18-month rate locks for Q4 renewals.
3. Paused mid-market acquisition; restructured sales team to focus exclusively on enterprise ($100K+ ACV) where differentiation remains defensible.

**Q4 Outlook**

Revised guidance: $4.8M revenue (flat YoY). Recovery to growth trajectory expected Q1 2027 contingent on enterprise pipeline conversion (4 deals, $2.4M total value, 70% weighted probability).

**Request:** Board approval for $300K emergency retention budget to secure at-risk accounts.
