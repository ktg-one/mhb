### 1. Explain blockchain to a 10-year-old.

Imagine you and your friends share a notebook. Every time someone trades a sticker, everyone writes it down in their own copy of the notebook. No one can sneak in and change a page without everyone noticing, because all the notebooks have to match. That’s blockchain: a shared record that no single person controls and no one can secretly alter.
