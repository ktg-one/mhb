**Target locked. Mission objective: 18-month trajectory forecast for this 3-founder, $200K-seed B2B dental SaaS launching in Perth with 12 beta users.**

End state defined. We optimize for **survivability and capital efficiency**, not founder feelings or “hustle porn.” What must be true in 18 months (July 2027) for this to be anything other than another statistic? Cash-positive runway extension, repeatable sales motion, and a path to $150K–$300K ARR minimum — or a clean pivot/acqui-hire before the tank hits empty. Anything less is drift, and drift is execution failure.

Here is the ruthless systems-level projection, worked backward from the data (90% overall startup failure rate; SaaS-specific 38%+ failure by month 32; 74% of recent shutdowns at pre-seed/seed stage; Australian dental PMS market only ~$55M today scaling to $102M by 2030 at 11% CAGR; ~21,000 national dental practices but entrenched incumbents like Centaur/Dental4Windows already own the local stack).

### Runway Reality Check (Month 0–6)
$200K seed in Perth 2026 is pocket change. Realistic founder burn (modest salaries + AWS + legal + marketing) = $25K–$35K/month. That’s 6–8 months before you’re personally funding payroll or diluting at a fire-sale valuation.  
12 betas mean zero proven PMF. Dentists are conservative; sales cycles run 3–9 months. Conversion to paid will be brutal unless your differentiation is surgical (not “we have AI notes”).

**Most probable outcome at Month 6:** 4–8 paying users, <$8K MRR, 60–70% cash burned. Still pre-revenue scale. This is the first decision gate. Miss it and the mission is already compromised.

### 18-Month Scenarios (Probability-Weighted, No Mercy)
**Base Case – 70–75% Probability: Controlled Shutdown or Zombie Mode**  
Cash runs dry by Month 12–14. You’ve converted maybe 20–30 dentists nationally (Perth caps you hard; the real TAM is national but acquisition costs explode outside WA). Churn hits 15–25% because dentists hate change and your feature parity with incumbents shows. No Series A interest (median seed now $1M+ in Aus; investors demand 3–5x YoY growth at this stage). Outcome: graceful wind-down, founders take consulting gigs or acqui-hire scraps. Total loss: your time + investor $200K. Classic “no market need” or “ran out of runway” failure (42% and 29% of startup deaths).

**Optimistic Case – 15–20% Probability: Scraping to Seed Extension / Small Raise**  
You nail a repeatable outbound + dental-association playbook. Hit $40K–$80K ARR by Month 18 with <20% churn and LTV:CAC >3:1. National expansion starts (QLD/NSW pilots). You raise a $500K–$1M angel/bridge round at $3–5M pre. Still tiny, but alive. Requires: founder sales gods, product that saves dentists 5+ hours/week, and zero lifestyle creep.

**Dominant Case – 5–10% Probability: Real Escape Velocity**  
You own the niche. $150K+ ARR, 40%+ MoM early growth stabilizing to 15–20% MoM, NRR >110%. You become the “Centaur killer” for independent practices. Perth launch proves concept; you expand via partnerships. Raise proper Series A at $8–12M valuation. This is the only path that matters. Everything else is noise.

### What Must Be True (Non-Negotiable Milestones)
1. **Month 3:** 8/12 betas convert to paid at $99–$299/mo. Gross churn <10%. Clear ICP validated (solo vs. group practices).
2. **Month 6:** $15K MRR, CAC payback <12 months, runway >9 months remaining. National sales motion documented (not just warm intros).
3. **Month 12:** $60K+ MRR or proven 3x growth from Month 6. Cash in bank or committed follow-on.
4. **Month 18:** Positive unit economics, defensibility (moat via integrations or AI workflow only you own), and a narrative investors can’t ignore.

Miss any gate and you execute a controlled pivot or exit. No heroic “just one more feature” drift.

### Execution Plan – The Only Bets Worth Taking
- **Ruthless focus:** Kill everything not moving paid conversion. Outbound to every WA dentist first (small market advantage). Price for speed, not perfection.
- **Capital discipline:** Founders on minimum viable salary until $10K MRR. Bootstrap the rest. Perth cost of living is your edge — use it.
- **Differentiation or die:** Incumbents already do billing/scheduling. Your only shot is something that genuinely reduces chair-time or compliance risk. Prove it with data or pivot to services/white-label.
- **Exit ramps pre-built:** Line up 2–3 dental software acquirers now. If traction stalls, sell the user list/IP for 2–3x cash returned.
