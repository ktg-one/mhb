### The 18-Month Trajectory: Three Scenarios

#### 🟢 Best Case (20% probability): $45K MRR, Seed Extension
- **Conversion:** 30% of beta → paid ($299/mo avg) = ~4 paying in Month 3
- **Growth:** 8-12 new dental practices/mo via referrals (dentists trust peers) [[Grand View Research]]
- **Revenue by Month 18:** ~$45K MRR ($540K ARR)
- **Funding:** Raise $750K-1.2M seed extension at $4-6M cap (Perth angels, healthtech focused)
- **Team:** Hire 1 part-time support, founders still technical
- **Key Success Factor:** Solved a *specific* pain (e.g., insurance claims automation) not "another practice management tool"

#### 🟡 Base Case (55% probability): $12K MRR, Bootstrap or Stall
- **Conversion:** 10-15% of beta → paid = 1-2 paying in Month 3
- **Growth:** 2-4 new practices/mo via slow word-of-mouth
- **Revenue by Month 18:** ~$12K MRR ($144K ARR)
- **Funding:** Unable to raise; founders take minimal salary or side contracts
- **Team:** No hires; founder burnout risk high
- **Reality:** This is the median outcome for Perth B2B SaaS with <$500K seed [[GetLatka]]

#### 🔴 Worst Case (25% probability): < $3K MRR, Shutdown
- **Conversion:** <5% of beta → paid; churn >10%/mo
- **Growth:** Stuck at 12-15 total users; CAC > LTV
- **Revenue by Month 18:** <$3K MRR
- **Outcome:** Founders pivot, shut down, or get acqui-hired by established player (e.g., Henry Schein, Carestream) [[Grand View Research]]
- **Root Cause:** Built a "nice-to-have" in a market that only buys "must-have"

### Critical Perth-Specific Factors
1. **Market Size Constraint:** WA has ~1,200 dental practices. Even 10% penetration = 120 customers. At $300/mo, that's $36K MRR ceiling *in WA*. Expansion to Eastern States is mandatory for scale—but adds complexity.
2. **Sales Cycle:** Dentists are conservative. B2B healthcare SaaS in Australia averages 3-6 month sales cycles. Your $200K seed may not cover 2 full cycles.
3. **Competition:** Global players (Curve Dental, Dentrix) and local (NetDent, DentalTap) already serve this market [[SoftwareSuggest]]. Differentiation must be razor-sharp.
4. **Talent:** Perth's SaaS ecosystem has ~381 employees across 25 companies [[GetLatka]]. Hiring experienced B2B SaaS talent is hard and expensive.

### Strategic Recommendations (If This Is Your Venture)
1. **Niche Down Further:** Don't build "SaaS for dentists." Build "automated insurance reconciliation for Perth orthodontists." Specificity beats generality in conservative markets.
2. **Pre-Sell Before Building:** Get 5 LOIs at $X/mo before writing more code. Dentists buy outcomes, not features.
3. **Leverage Local Programs:** Apply to Perth Biodesign if healthtech-adjacent—it provides clinical validation and investor access [[VentureUWA]].
4. **Plan for Eastern States at Month 6:** WA alone cannot support venture-scale returns. Build a remote sales playbook early.
5. **Crypto-Agile Architecture:** Given your quantum security focus, ensure any patient data handling is PQC-ready. "Harvest Now, Decrypt Later" applies to healthcare data too.

### The Anthropological Lens (Team LLM)
Dentists are not early adopters. They are *risk mitigators*. Your product must reduce perceived risk, not add cognitive load. This is not a technology sale—it is a trust sale.

In Perth, trust is built face-to-face. Digital-only GTM will underperform. Budget 40% of runway for in-person demos, dental association sponsorships, and local conferences.

**Bottom Line:** With $200K seed, 3 founders, and 12 beta users in Perth, the most likely 18-month outcome is **$10-15K MRR, founder fatigue, and a hard decision: raise, pivot, or shut down.**

The window to change that trajectory is *now*—not at Month 12.
