- Verify every AI‑generated fact or number against a primary source before presenting it to stakeholders.

- Treat AI as a drafting or brainstorming assistant, not as a trusted source of truth.

- Maintain human oversight (peer review, validation) for any AI‑derived content that will be relied upon for decisions.

- Accept personal accountability for the accuracy of information you present, regardless of its origin.

- Clearly label uncertain or unverified information and avoid presenting AI output as definitive fact.
