Based on current Perth market conditions and standard B2B SaaS failure/success vectors, here is the 18-month projection for this dental startup.

### 1. The Burn Rate Trap

A **$200K seed** for **3 founders** is extremely lean.

- **Wages:** Even at a modest **$60K/year** per founder, salary alone consumes **$180K** in 12 months.
    
- **Result:** Unless they are working for equity only or have hit **Product-Market Fit (PMF)** within 6 months, they will run out of cash by month 10. By month 18, they are either closed or surviving on "lifestyle business" revenue.
    

### 2. The Perth Market Constraint

Perth is a geographically isolated "village" market.

- **The "Twelve Beta" Ceiling:** 12 beta users is a promising start, but the dental market in WA is finite. To scale beyond a hobby, they must move past the "early adopter" phase into the "pragmatist" market quickly.
    
- **Sales Cycle:** Medical and dental B2B cycles are notoriously slow (3–9 months). Decisions are often made based on trust and local reputation rather than pure technical superiority.
    
- **Outcome:** By 18 months, they will have either dominated the local WA independent clinics or realized that the **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)** for East Coast expansion is too high for their remaining capital.
    

### 3. The Technical/Operational Wall

- **Legacy Integration:** Most dental clinics use entrenched software (e.g., Exact, D4W). If this SaaS does not offer seamless integration, the churn rate at the 12-month mark will spike as "manual entry fatigue" sets in.
    
- **The Pivot:** By month 18, a successful team will likely have pivoted from a "broad" tool to a "specific" pain point—like automated AI-driven patient re-engagement or specialized Medicare/Health fund reconciliation.
    

### 18-Month Probability Table

|**Probability**|**Outcome**|**Primary Reason**|
|---|---|---|
|**50%**|**Dead / Stagnant**|Ran out of cash before achieving scalable revenue; founders returned to corporate roles.|
|**35%**|**Zombies**|Revenue covers costs but lacks growth. 50–100 local users. High stress, low salary.|
|**10%**|**Series A / Growth**|Achieved **$30K+ MRR** (Monthly Recurring Revenue). Expanding to Melbourne/Sydney.|
|**5%**|**Acquired**|A larger practice management player buys them for the talent or the niche feature.|

### Critical Advice for the Founders

1. **Cut Salaries:** If they are drawing full pay from $200K, the math fails.
    
2. **Monetize Early:** Move those 12 beta users to paid accounts by month 4 to prove **willingness to pay**.
    
3. **The "Perth Moat":** Use the local isolation to build a "fortress" of testimonials before attempting the East Coast.
