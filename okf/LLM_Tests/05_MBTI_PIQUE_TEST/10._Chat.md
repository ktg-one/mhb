**1. Explain blockchain to a 10-year-old**

Imagine a notebook shared by lots of people.  
Every time someone writes a new transaction in it, everyone else gets the same update.

That notebook is hard to cheat with, because:

- lots of people have a copy
    
- new entries are added in order
    
- once something is written and checked, changing it is very hard
    

So blockchain is basically a shared digital record book that many computers help protect.

**2. Draft a board memo about Q3 revenue decline**

**Subject: Q3 Revenue Decline Overview and Immediate Response**

Board Members,

Q3 revenue came in below plan, driven by a mix of slower new customer acquisition, longer sales cycles, and softer expansion revenue from existing accounts. While the decline is meaningful, the underlying picture is more specific than structural collapse. Our core demand remains present, but conversion efficiency weakened and several expected deals shifted out of quarter.

The main contributing factors were concentrated in three areas. First, pipeline velocity slowed, particularly in mid-market accounts where decision-making cycles lengthened. Second, upsell performance underdelivered against forecast, largely because customer adoption in key segments lagged behind expectations. Third, a small number of delayed renewals had an outsized impact on quarterly results due to deal concentration.

Management is taking immediate action in four areas. We are tightening forecast discipline, increasing scrutiny on late-stage pipeline assumptions, prioritizing faster-converting segments, and putting focused attention on customer expansion risk. In parallel, we are reviewing pricing, packaging, and onboarding friction to improve conversion and retention performance heading into Q4.

At this stage, the decline appears correctable, but not ignorable. We will return with a revised forecast, a clearer account-level recovery plan, and leading indicators the board can track more reliably over the next quarter.
