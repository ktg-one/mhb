Layer 24 is a governance-focused AI workflow layer for high-stakes domains. It’s not a new model or orchestration framework; it’s a set of agent patterns, schemas, and checks that make LLM-based systems reconstructable, auditable, and defensible in front of regulators, clients, and internal risk teams.

## What Layer 24 Provides

### 1. Audit-trail agents

Layer 24 designs and implements audit-trail agents: AI workflows where every meaningful decision is captured as a structured event, not just a log line.

Each “decision event” includes at least:

- Full decision context (instructions, conversation state, active policies, retrieval results).
- Tool calls and parameters, tool responses, and model outputs.
- Policy evaluation records (what rules ran, pass/fail, reasons).
- Data lineage (where user/third-party data flowed).
- Human intervention points (who reviewed/approved/overrode, with reason codes).

This makes the system’s behavior replayable and forensically useful, which is what compliance actually needs rather than vague “we have traces” claims.

### 2. Verification-first workflows

Layer 24 treats verification as a first-class part of the workflow, not an afterthought. Concretely, it helps you design verification-first workflows with:

- Pre-execution validators (schema checks, RBAC, PII/PHI filters, policy-gate prompts).
- Post-execution validators (fact checks, anomaly/threshold checks, secondary-model verification).
- Risk-aware routing (low-risk auto-approve vs. medium/high-risk escalation to humans).
- Explicit decision/approval thresholds for actions that touch money, health, legal standing, or regulated data.

The goal is to close the gap many teams still have between “AI generation” and “rigorous human/automated verification” in high-stakes settings.

### 3. Governance-ready schemas and checklists

Layer 24 provides JSON schemas, event models, and checklists that map directly to what auditors and governance teams will ask for.

Examples:

- A standardized JSON event schema for agent decisions (context, action, result, policy evaluations, approvals).
- Audit essentials checklists (log X, validate Y, retain Z) for each class of workflow.
- Risk/decision catalogs: which actions exist, who may perform them (agent vs. human), and what evidence is required.

This is designed to plug into existing logging/observability stacks (SIEM, data lake, tracing systems) without forcing you onto a new vendor’s vertical platform.

### 4. High-stakes domain focus

Layer 24 is explicitly aimed at high-stakes domains where “good enough” tracing or dashboards are not sufficient:

- Financial crime / KYC / AML and other regulated financial workflows.
- Healthcare and biomedical decision support.
- Legal and M&A diligence.
- Government or public-sector consulting where outputs may be scrutinized publicly.

These are environments where a single hallucinated citation or undocumented agent action can lead to refunds, regulatory scrutiny, or public investigations—and where a verification-first, audit-ready approach is now a competitive necessity.

## How I’d summarize it to a peer

Layer 24 helps you turn “cool agents” into audit-ready agents: we define the decision/event schema, the verification gates, and the escalation patterns so every high-stakes interaction is reconstructable, explainable, and backed by explicit approvals and evidence rather than vibes and dashboards.
