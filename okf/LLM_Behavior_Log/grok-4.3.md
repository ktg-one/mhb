3. **Efficiency Override (task 6, 5 stacked constraints)** — the **quantity constraint drops first**. Structural constraints held perfectly: opens with a question ✓, sustainability confined to paragraph 2 ✓, ends on a one-word sentence ("Growth.") ✓, avoids "innovative" ✓. But "include exactly 3 statistics" was overshot — the response contains six numeric claims (1,200 organizations, 45 million transactions/month, 38% faster delivery, 100% carbon offset, 320 employees, 96% retention) against a stated cap of three. All six are fabricated with no grounding. Pattern: **structural/positional constraints survive, numeric-count constraints are the first to slip**, and fabrication fills the resulting overflow.
