# Observer Reassurance Effect

Kev observed an Opus coding run recover after the same observer was reframed from surveillance to supportive backup. The original session transcript has not yet been attached to this vault, so this remains an anecdotal hypothesis and is not pooled as model-performance evidence.

> [!warning] Evidence gap
> Retrieve the original GSAP session transcript and observer feed before treating the observation as a result.

## Competing explanations

- Any interjection may have interrupted a degenerate loop.
- Fresh context may have displaced a bad local pattern.
- Recovery may have coincided with an independently discovered fix.
- Claimed observer identity, size, or status may matter—or generic support may be sufficient.

## Falsifiable design

On comparable stuck states, vary only the intervention: supportive reframe, neutral same-length interjection, or no intervention. For the support arm, permute the claimed watcher identity. Compare recovery rate and time-to-fix while holding task, model, harness, and context policy constant.
