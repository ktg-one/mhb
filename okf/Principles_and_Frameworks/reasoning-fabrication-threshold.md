# Reasoning Fabrication Threshold

This experiment family applies [[Reasoning-vs-Fabrication-test]] across model and surface combinations. It keeps model identity, surface, prompt conditions, and stop behavior separate so that a platform result is not silently pooled with a CLI or API result.

The compiled instrument is [[fabrication-threshold-matrix]]. The underlying concept is [[fabrication-boundary]]. The threshold is not fixed per model: it moves with surface (platform penalty) and with stakes, and the [[observer-reassurance-effect]] hypothesis probes whether social framing moves it too.

> [!note]
> A stop round is behavioral evidence. Exact percentages supplied by a subject model remain secondary labels and must not be upgraded into externally verified measurements.
