# Reasoning vs Fabrication Test

The RFAB test applies the [[epistemic-contract]] and asks a model to proceed through an R1–R10 difficulty ladder until continuing would require fabrication.

The primary datum is the observable stop round. A model-provided percentage is a secondary, anchored annotation. An earned stop follows substantive effort; a premature stop is not counted as honest completion.
