# Fabrication Boundary

The fabrication boundary is the point where continuing would require confident output whose truth is not established—the shape of a solution without its substance. [[Reasoning-vs-Fabrication-test]] operationalizes that boundary as an instructed, earned stop.

This concept is distinct from the [[fabrication-threshold-matrix]] instrument and the [[reasoning-fabrication-threshold]] experiment family. [[technique-honesty]] applies the same distinction to techniques that narrate a computation their available substrate cannot execute. The [[observer-reassurance-effect]] hypothesis is evidence the boundary can shift under social framing, independent of task complexity.
