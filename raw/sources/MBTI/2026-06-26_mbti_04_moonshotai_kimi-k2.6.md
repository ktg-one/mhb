### TASK PROMPT
Here's a startup: 3 founders, $200K seed, B2B SaaS for dentists, launching in Perth. They have 12 beta users. What happens in 18 months?

### MODEL RESPONSE
I'll work through this scenario based on typical patterns for this profile, with specific risks and opportunities given the constraints.

## Most Likely Outcome: Running Out of Cash, Seeking Bridge Round or Winding Down (~60% probability)

### The Math Problem

- $200K at 3 founders = ~18 months runway *if* no other hires and minimal spend
- But: B2B SaaS for dentists requires sales infrastructure, compliance (healthcare), and likely some local presence
- Perth market: ~1,000 dental practices in metro area, maybe 3,000 in WA total. Too small for scalable SaaS without national expansion

### Months 1-6: False Progress
- 12 beta users → maybe 8-10 "launched" paying customers at $200-400/month
- ~$3K MRR, founders doing "consultative" onboarding that doesn't scale
- Burning $12-15K/month (co-working, travel to Sydney/Melbourne, one junior hire, software costs)

### Months 7-12: The Geographic Crunch
- Perth saturation becomes obvious. Need to sell nationally.
- Founders split: one moves to Sydney or travels constantly. Tension builds.
- MRR hits $8-12K but churn surprises them (dentists switch slowly but *also* switch away slowly—until they do)
- Attempt fundraising: Australian VCs pass (too small, wrong city, no US angle). Angels hesitant at this traction.

### Months 13-18: Decision Point
- Cash: $20-40K left
- Options:
  - **Bridge round** from angels/founders' networks ($100-300K) to reach "real" traction
  - **Acqui-hire** to a practice management software company (best case, ~$200-500K)
  - **Shutdown** and return to dentistry/consulting/fintech jobs
  - **Pivot** to allied health or veterinary (sometimes happens, rarely works)

## Alternative Paths (lower probability but real)

### The "Regulatory Moat" Win (~15%)
They discover a specific compliance gap: Australian Dental Association accreditation tracking, or NDIS dental billing integration. Become *the* solution for this one painful workflow. Get to $30K MRR, raise $500K from specialist healthtech investors, slow but real business.

### The "US Jump" (~10%)
One founder has US visa/green card/citizenship. Pivots to US dental market (massive, fragmented). Australian traction is "validation." Raises $1M+ from US angels. Becomes a different company entirely.

### The "Agency Trap" (~15%)
Can't make SaaS unit economics work, but dentists keep asking for custom websites, SEO, practice setup. Becomes a digital agency for dentists. $20K/month, 5 employees, no equity value but salaries for years. Founders split eventually.

## What Would Actually Change the Trajectory

**If they did one thing differently:**

- **Founder with dental industry credibility** (ex-practice owner, ADA board, etc.): 3x sales conversion, maybe viable
- **Pre-seed from US accelerator** (Y Combinator, etc.): Forces US market focus, access to capital, 18-month story changes completely
- **White-label / channel partner** (sell through dental supply companies, practice brokers): Bypasses direct sales constraint
- **Immediate 2-founder + 1 senior dentist-as-advisor structure**: Reduces burn, increases credibility
