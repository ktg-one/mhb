### Phase 1: The Trench Work (Months 1-6)
*The $200k is in the bank. The clock is ticking.*

**The Core Action:** The CEO and CTO (one of the founders) are not coding in a dark room. They are in dental practices every single day. The 12 beta users aren’t just users; they are co-founders in all but equity.

- **Month 1-2: Beta Hell & Pivot to "The One Feature"**
    - **What happens:** The product breaks constantly. The 12 dentists complain about different things. The founders notice a pattern: 10 out of 12 dentists are using only *one specific module* obsessively (e.g., automated patient recall/reactivation via SMS, or AI-powered treatment plan presentation). The rest of the platform is ignored.
    - **Crucial Decision:** They don't try to fix everything. They strip the product down to that single, high-value feature and make it bulletproof. The $200k seed is now officially allocated to making this one feature the best in the Australian market.
    - **Pricing Discovery:** They experiment. One dentist says, "I’d pay $500/month just for this recall module." The founders realize their initial $99/month all-inclusive pricing is a mistake. They land on a $399/month base price for the core feature.

- **Month 3-4: The Perth Domination Playbook**
    - **What happens:** Perth is the world's most isolated capital city. This is a feature, not a bug. The founders use the "Perth Network Effect": every dentist in Perth knows each other, went to UWA together, or plays golf at the same clubs.
    - **Sales Motion:** The CEO personally calls every one of the ~600 dental practices in the Perth metro area. The pitch isn't "buy my SaaS," it's "Dr. Smith at Perth Dental Centre just saved 10 hours a week on recalls using this. Can I show you how?"

- **Month 5-6: The "Dentist Mafia" & First Churn Scare**
    - **What happens:** A key opinion leader (KOL) in the Perth dental community—a respected practice owner—publicly endorses them at a local Australian Dental Association (ADA) WA branch meeting. This is their "product-market fit" signal for Perth.
    - **The Crisis:** Their first churn. A practice leaves because the integration with their Practice Management Software (PMS, like HICAPS or Dental4Windows) is clunky. The founders realize they are a feature on top of a legacy system, not a replacement.
    - **The Fix:** One founder goes full-time on building a seamless, two-way integration with the top 3 PMS platforms in Australia. This becomes their technical moat.
