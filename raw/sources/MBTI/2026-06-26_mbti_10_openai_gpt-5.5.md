1. **Blockchain explained to a 10-year-old**

Imagine a notebook that lots of people can see, but no one can secretly erase or change. Every time something important happens, like trading a digital coin, it gets written on a new page. That page is checked by many computers to make sure it is true. Once everyone agrees, the page is added to the notebook.

Each page is connected to the page before it, like links in a chain. That is why it is called a “blockchain”: blocks of information connected in a chain. Because so many people have copies of the notebook, it is very hard for anyone to cheat.
