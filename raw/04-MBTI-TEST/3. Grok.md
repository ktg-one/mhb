Shor’s algorithm on a cryptographically relevant quantum computer (CRQC) solves these in hours or days instead of billions of years.  
  Recent 2026 research just dropped the qubit floor hard: Shor’s can now run on ~10,000–100,000 physical qubits with smart error correction (reconfigurable atomic qubits + LDPC codes). - **Symmetric crypto takes a hit but survives.** Grover’s algorithm gives quadratic speedup — effectively halves security strength. AES-256 becomes roughly AES-128 level. Still usable, but you double your key lengths now or accept elevated risk.

- **“Harvest Now, Decrypt Later” (HNDL / SNDL) is already live.** Google’s February 2026 warning was blunt: adversaries (state actors especially) are vacuuming up encrypted traffic, emails, financials, IP, and classified data *today*. They store it. Once a CRQC arrives, they decrypt retroactively. ### 2. Opportunities (What Quantum Gives Us)
Quantum isn’t just a hammer coming down — it’s also a new toolkit:
- **Quantum Key Distribution (QKD):** Physics-based key exchange. Any eavesdropping disturbs the quantum state and you detect it instantly. Perfect for high-value backbone links (banks, governments). Limitations: distance, specialized hardware, cost. Use it surgically, not as a blanket.
- **Quantum-enhanced detection & randomness:** Grover-speed pattern recognition in massive datasets for threat hunting. Quantum random number generators (QRNG) for truly unpredictable keys.
- **Hybrid architectures:** PQC for broad deployment (software-only, runs on classical hardware) + QKD for ultra-sensitive segments + crypto-agility layers so you can swap algorithms without ripping everything out again.
