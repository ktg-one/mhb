# 📊 AI Anthropology — Research & OKF Dashboard

Welcome to your central control plane for AI Anthropology research, LLM diagnostic experiments, and Open Knowledge Format (OKF) knowledge base assets.

---

> [!info] How Obsidian Bases & This Dashboard Work
> 1. **Live Dynamic Database**: This dashboard uses Obsidian's native `.base` engine (`Dashboards/Bases/ai-anthropology.base`). It queries the vault in real-time — as notes are created, moved, or tagged in `/okf`, this table updates automatically.
> 2. **Multi-View Tabs**: You can switch between different tabs at the top of the base view:
>    - **All OKF Knowledge Assets**: Overview of every structured note in `/okf`.
>    - **Principles & Frameworks**: Visual card gallery for theoretical models like the [[SCCD_Model|SCCD Model]] and [[Efficiency_Mandate_Transparency|Efficiency Mandate]].
>    - **LLM Diagnostic Tests**: Filtered table of reasoning, fabrication, and honesty diagnostics.
> 3. **Sorting & Filtering**: Click column headers to sort by title, last modified date, or OKF concept type.
